Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

How do I list my business?

Click "Submit Business" in the navigation menu, fill out the form with your business details, and submit. Your free baseline listing goes live immediately!

How do I list my church?

Use the "Submit Church" option to add your congregation to our directory. Churches are always free to list.

Do I need an account?

No account needed to browse. You'll need to create an account to submit listings, write reviews, or upgrade to Pro tier.

Free vs. Pro Tier

What's included in the free listing?

Free listings include:

  • Basic business information and contact details
  • Primary category selection
  • One secondary category
  • Reviews and ratings
  • Permanent listing (never deleted)

What does Pro tier add?

Pro tier ($9-12/month) includes everything in free, plus:

  • Up to 5 secondary categories
  • Photos and video
  • Featured placement in search results
  • QR code for offline marketing
  • Enhanced business profile
  • Priority support

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Your listing is never deleted. It simply downgrades to the free baseline tier. You can re-upgrade anytime.

Managing Your Listing

How do I edit my business information?

Log in to your Business Admin Dashboard to update your listing details, hours, photos, and more.

How do I respond to reviews?

Pro tier businesses can respond to reviews directly from their Business Admin Dashboard.

Can I feature my favorite businesses on my church page?

Yes! Church admins can feature member-owned businesses on their church profile page.

Payments & Billing

How does payment work?

We use Stripe for secure payment processing. Pro tier subscriptions renew automatically each month.

Can I get a refund?

We don't offer refunds for partial months, but you can cancel anytime to prevent future charges.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and digital payment methods through Stripe.

Platform Features

How do I search for businesses?

Use the search bar to find businesses by name, category, or location. Filter results to narrow your search.

How do reviews work?

Any user can leave a review for a business they've worked with. Reviews help the community make informed decisions.

What are the categories?

We offer 50+ business categories from Auto Repair to Web Design. Business owners can suggest new categories for admin approval.

Kingdom Connects Mission

What is the tithing model?

Kingdom Connects tithes 10% of gross revenue to support churches and Christian ministries. When you upgrade to Pro, you're helping fund Kingdom work!

Who can use Kingdom Connects?

Anyone can browse our directory. We welcome all Christian businesses and churches to join our platform.

Still Have Questions?

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us and we'll be happy to help!
