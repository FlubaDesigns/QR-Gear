🕍 Church Admin Questionnaire – Draft Concept

Here’s a solid first-pass list of questions to include.
These balance simplicity with insight — short enough that admins won’t bounce, but rich enough to build meaningful stats.

Basic Info

1. Church Name


2. Church Website (optional)


3. Church Email / Phone



Congregation Profile

4. Approximately how many active members attend your church? (number input)


5. How long has your church been established? (dropdown: <1 year, 1–5 years, 6–10, 10+)


6. How many weekly services or gatherings do you host? (number)


7. Do you have youth, men’s, or women’s ministries? (checkboxes)



Engagement & Influence

8. Does your church currently promote local businesses? (Yes/No)


9. Would you like to feature Kingdom Connects in your bulletin or website? (Yes/No)


10. Estimated number of people who might see Kingdom Connects promotions each week (e.g., bulletins, slides, or word of mouth). (number)



Optional

11. Who referred you to Kingdom Connects? (text)


12. Would you like a free Kingdom Connects welcome kit? (Yes/No)


13. Mailing address for welcome kit (if Yes).




---

📊 Data Use & Impact

Every response feeds into a churchMetrics collection in Firestore.

A simple field like estimated_audience_reach can aggregate total reach across all churches.
(E.g., 150 churches × avg. 120 attendees = ~18,000 potential viewers.)

You can later display a live counter on your site:

> “Kingdom Connects is now reaching an estimated 23,000 people through partnering churches.”




That’s credibility and momentum.
