About Kingdom Connects

Kingdom Connects is a faith-based business and church directory designed to help the Body of Christ support each other's businesses.

Our Mission

We exist to connect church members with local Christian businesses, driving more customers to faith-aligned entrepreneurs while strengthening community bonds. When believers support each other economically, everyone grows stronger.

How It Works

For Church Members:

  • Browse businesses by category and location
  • Find trusted, faith-aligned service providers
  • Support your church community through your purchases
  • Leave reviews to help others make informed decisions

For Business Owners:

  • List your business for free (always free for church members)
  • Upgrade to Pro for enhanced features ($9-12/month)
  • Connect with customers who value faith-aligned commerce
  • Your listing is permanent - never deleted based on payment status

Giving Back

10% of all Kingdom Connects revenue goes back to churches and outreach programs. This isn't just a business - it's a ministry. We believe in tithing first and building sustainable Christian commerce that supports the Kingdom.

Our Values

  • Church First: Churches and their members are the priority, not profit
  • Permanent Listings: We never delete listings - only downgrade if payments lapse
  • Transparency: Clear pricing, honest service, faithful stewardship
  • Community: Building connections that strengthen the Body of Christ
  • Integrity: Operating with Kingdom principles in all we do
