Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 8, 2024

Information We Collect

Kingdom Connects collects information to provide and improve our faith-based business directory services:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number when you create an account or submit a listing
  • Business Information: Business name, address, contact details, categories, and descriptions
  • Church Information: Church name, location, contact information, and affiliation details
  • Usage Data: Pages viewed, search queries, and interactions with our platform

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate and maintain the Kingdom Connects directory
  • Connect church members with Christian businesses
  • Process Pro tier subscriptions and payments
  • Send important updates about your listings or account
  • Improve our platform and user experience
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform security

Information Sharing

Kingdom Connects does not sell your personal information. We share information only in these circumstances:

  • Public Listings: Business and church information you submit is displayed publicly in our directory
  • Service Providers: We use Firebase (Google) for hosting and Stripe for payment processing
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using Firebase security protocols.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and download your data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Opt out of marketing communications

Cookies and Tracking

We use localStorage to save your theme preferences and display settings. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

Children's Privacy

Kingdom Connects is not directed to individuals under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
