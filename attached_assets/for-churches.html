Frequently Asked Questions

Is there any cost for churches to join?

No! Church profiles are 100% free, forever. There are no hidden fees, subscription costs, or charges of any kind.

How much revenue can my church earn?

It depends on how many businesses you feature and how many upgrade to Pro. If you feature 10 businesses and 5 upgrade to Pro at $10/month, your church receives a share of that $50/month ongoing. The more you promote, the more sustainable ministry funding you create.

Can we feature businesses outside our congregation?

Absolutely! Feature any Christian-owned business you trust and want to recommend to your members. They don't have to attend your church.

How do we receive revenue payments?

Monthly payments are sent via your preferred method (bank transfer, PayPal, or check). You'll set this up in your church admin dashboard after approval.

What denominations are eligible?

All Christian denominations are welcome! Whether you're Baptist, Methodist, Non-denominational, Catholic, Pentecostal, or any other Christian tradition, you can join Kingdom Connects.
