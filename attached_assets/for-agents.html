Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a cost to become an agent?

No! There's zero cost to apply and become a Kingdom Connects sales agent. We make money when you make money.

Do I need sales experience?

Not at all! We provide full training, marketing materials, and support. If you can build relationships and communicate value, you can succeed as a Kingdom Connects agent.

When do I get paid commissions?

Upfront bonuses are paid within 30 days of a Pro subscription starting. Recurring monthly commissions are paid on the 15th of each month for the prior month's subscriptions.

What if a client cancels their Pro subscription?

Your recurring commission stops when they cancel, but you keep all commissions earned while they were active. This is why building a large portfolio is important—some will cancel, but most stay long-term.

Can I do this part-time?

Absolutely! Many of our top agents started part-time and built up their client portfolio over months. Set your own schedule and work at your own pace.
