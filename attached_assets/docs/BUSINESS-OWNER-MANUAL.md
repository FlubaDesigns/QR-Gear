## Chapter 1: Getting Started

### Creating Your Business Listing

**Before you begin:**
- Have your business information ready (address, phone, hours, etc.)
- Know which church your business is affiliated with
- Decide on your primary business category
- Have your logo/photos ready (if upgrading to Pro)

**Step-by-step process:**

1. **Get Business Owner Role**
   - Contact the Platform Admin via email
   - Request business-admin role upgrade
   - Provide: Business name, your name, email, phone
   - Wait for admin approval (usually 24-48 hours)

2. **Submit Your Business Listing**
   - Go to: kingdomconnects.com/submit-business.html
   - Or navigate to: Dashboard → My Listing → Edit
   - Fill in all required fields (see below)
   - Click **Submit for Approval**

3. **Wait for Approval**
   - Admin reviews your submission
   - Typical approval time: 1-3 business days
   - You'll receive an email when approved
   - Your listing goes live immediately upon approval

---

### Required vs Optional Information

#### ✅ REQUIRED FIELDS (Must Complete)

**Business Basics:**
- **Business Name** - Your registered business name
- **Your Name** - Primary contact person
- **Email** - Business email (customers will see this)
- **Phone** - Business phone (customers will see this)

**Address:**
- **Street Address** (Line 1) - Physical location
- **City** - Must match your state
- **State** - 2-letter code (e.g., FL, TX, CA)
- **ZIP Code** - 5-digit postal code

**Classification:**
- **Primary Category** - Choose ONE main category
- **Church Affiliation** - Select your church from dropdown

#### 📋 OPTIONAL FIELDS (Recommended)

**Contact Information:**
- **Address Line 2** - Suite, unit, building number
- **Website** - Your business website URL
- **Description** - Tell customers about your business (max 500 characters)

**Hours of Operation:**
- Monday through Sunday
- For each day: Opening time, Closing time
- Check "Closed" for days you don't operate

**Privacy Settings:**
- Show/hide phone number publicly
- Show/hide email publicly
- Show/hide full address publicly
- Show/hide website publicly

## Chapter 2: Managing Your Listing

### Editing Business Information

**To update your listing:**
1. Log in to your dashboard
2. Click **My Listing** in the left menu
3. Make your changes
4. Scroll to bottom and click **Save Changes**
5. Changes appear immediately (no re-approval needed)

**What you can edit anytime:**
- Contact information (email, phone, website)
- Business description
- Hours of operation
- Privacy settings (show/hide contact info)
- Categories (within your tier limit)

**What requires admin approval:**
- Business name changes
- Address changes (relocation)
- Church affiliation changes

---

### Adding Photos and Videos (Pro Only)

**Free Tier:** No photos or videos allowed

**Pro Tier:**
- Up to 10 photos
- Up to 3 videos
- Maximum file size: 10MB per file
- Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, MP4, WebM

**How to upload photos:**
1. Upgrade to Pro (see Chapter 4)
2. Go to Dashboard → My Listing
3. Scroll to "Photos" section
4. Click **Upload Photos**
5. Drag and drop files or click to browse
6. Wait for upload to complete
7. Rearrange order by dragging thumbnails
8. Click **Save Changes**

**How to upload videos:**
1. Scroll to "Videos" section
2. Click **Upload Video**
3. Choose MP4 or WebM file (max 10MB)
4. Wait for upload (may take 1-2 minutes)
5. Click **Save Changes**

**Photo tips:**
- Use high-quality images (at least 1200px wide)
- First photo is your primary image (shows in search results)
- Include: Logo, storefront, products, team, work samples
- Avoid: Blurry photos, watermarks, text-heavy images

**Video tips:**
- Keep videos under 2 minutes
- Introduce yourself and your business
- Show your work/products in action
- Add subtitles for accessibility

---

### Updating Hours and Contact Info

**Hours of Operation:**
1. Dashboard → My Listing → Hours section
2. For each day of the week:
   - Select opening time (e.g., 9:00 AM)
   - Select closing time (e.g., 5:00 PM)
   - Check "Closed" if you don't operate that day
3. Click **Save Changes**

**Special hours (holidays, events):**
- Currently not supported
- Add a note in your business description
- Example: "Closed Thanksgiving and Christmas"

**Contact Information:**
- Email and phone update instantly
- Website URL is validated (must start with http:// or https://)
- Invalid URLs will show an error

---

### Privacy Settings

Control what information is visible to the public:

**Show Phone Number:**
- ✅ ON: Customers can see and click to call
- ❌ OFF: Phone hidden, customers use contact form

**Show Email:**
- ✅ ON: Customers can see and click to email
- ❌ OFF: Email hidden, customers use contact form

**Show Full Address:**
- ✅ ON: Street address visible, Google Maps link shown
- ❌ OFF: Only city and state shown

**Show Website:**
- ✅ ON: Website link visible and clickable
- ❌ OFF: Website hidden from public

**Recommended settings:**
- Show Phone: ON (easy for customers to contact you)
- Show Email: ON (multiple contact options)
- Show Full Address: ON (customers need to find you)
- Show Website: ON (drives traffic to your site)

## Chapter 3: Reviews and Ratings

### How Customers Submit Reviews

**Review System** (Coming Soon):
- Customers create an account
- Find your business listing
- Click "Write a Review"
- Rate 1-5 stars
- Write review text (max 1000 characters)
- Submit for approval

**Admin moderates reviews:**
- Checks for spam, profanity, inappropriate content
- Approves legitimate reviews
- Rejects fake or abusive reviews

---

### Responding to Reviews (Pro Only)

**Free Tier:** You can see reviews but cannot respond

**Pro Tier:**  
- Respond to any review on your listing
- Show appreciation for positive reviews
- Address concerns in negative reviews professionally
- Build trust with potential customers

**How to respond:**
1. Dashboard → Reviews
2. Find the review you want to respond to
3. Click **Respond**
4. Write your response (max 500 characters)
5. Click **Submit Response**
6. Response appears below the customer's review

**Response tips:**
- Thank customers for positive reviews
- Address specific points they mentioned
- For negative reviews: apologize, explain, offer to make it right
- Stay professional and Christ-like
- Don't argue or get defensive

---

### Managing Review Notifications

**Email notifications:**
1. Dashboard → Settings
2. Find "Notifications" section
3. Toggle "Email me when I receive a new review"
4. Choose notification frequency:
   - Instant (as soon as review is approved)
   - Daily digest (once per day)
   - Weekly digest (once per week)
   - Off (no email notifications)

**Dashboard notifications:**
- New reviews show a badge on the "Reviews" menu item
- Count resets when you view the Reviews page

---

### Understanding Your Average Rating

**How ratings are calculated:**
- Average of all approved reviews
- Scale: 1.0 to 5.0 stars
- Displayed with one decimal place (e.g., 4.7)
- Updates automatically when new reviews are approved

**Where your rating appears:**
- Business listing page (large, prominent display)
- Search results (next to your business name)
- Church member directory (if featured)
- Dashboard summary

**No reviews yet?**
- "No reviews yet" displays instead of stars
- Encourage happy customers to leave reviews
- Provide excellent service to earn 5-star reviews

**Low rating?**
- Respond professionally to negative reviews
- Address issues and improve service
- Ask satisfied customers to leave positive reviews
- One bad review won't ruin you - focus on the next 10 great ones

### What Happens If You Cancel?

**Kingdom Connects Permanent Listing Policy:**

Your listing is **NEVER deleted**, even if you stop paying. Here's what happens:

**When you cancel Pro:**
1. Billing stops immediately
2. Current billing period continues (you keep Pro until period ends)
3. At period end, listing downgrades to Free tier
4. Photos, videos, social links become hidden (not deleted)
5. Extra categories removed (reverts to 1 primary + 1 secondary)
6. Analytics access removed (data saved, viewable if you re-upgrade)

**Your listing remains active:**
- Business name, address, phone, email still visible
- Customers can still find and contact you
- Reviews and ratings remain unchanged
- Church affiliation stays active
- No interruption to customer access

**Re-upgrading:**
- Click "Upgrade to Pro" anytime
- All your hidden photos/videos reappear instantly
- Extra categories can be re-added
- Analytics history is preserved

**Why we do this:**
- Your business is part of the Kingdom economy
- We don't penalize you for financial hardship
- You can always return when ready
- Permanent support for Christian businesses

## Chapter 5: Categories and Suggestions

### Understanding the Category System

**Categories help customers find you:**
- Primary category: Your main business type
- Secondary categories: Additional services you offer
- Search filters: Customers browse by category
- Church directories: Churches sort members by category

**Dynamic system:**
- Categories are stored in the database (not hardcoded)
- Admins can add new categories
- Business owners can suggest categories
- Approval process ensures quality

---

### How to Suggest a New Category

**When to suggest a category:**
- Your business type isn't listed
- You offer a unique service not covered
- Multiple businesses need the same new category
- Industry-specific category would help customers

**Step-by-step process:**

1. **Go to Category Suggestions Page**
   - Dashboard → Category Suggestions (left menu)

2. **Click "Suggest New Category"**
   - Opens suggestion form

3. **Fill in Required Fields:**
   - **Suggested Category Name** - Be specific and professional
     - Good: "Mobile Auto Detailing"
     - Bad: "Car Stuff"
   - **Why is this needed?** - Explain how it helps
     - Example: "There are several mobile detailing businesses in our church network, and customers specifically search for mobile services vs traditional car washes."
   - **What businesses fit this category?** - Provide examples
     - Example: "Mobile detailing, mobile car wash, on-site auto cleaning services"

4. **Click "Submit Suggestion"**
   - Suggestion goes to admin queue
   - You receive confirmation message

5. **Wait for Admin Review**
   - Typical review time: 3-7 business days
   - Admin may approve, modify, or deny

6. **Receive Notification**
   - Email notification when admin makes decision
   - If approved: Category appears in your dropdown immediately
   - If denied: Explanation provided, you can re-submit with changes

---

### Category Approval Process

**What admins consider:**
- Is this a legitimate business category?
- Are there multiple businesses that fit this category?
- Is it too broad or too narrow?
- Does it overlap with existing categories?
- Is the name professional and clear?

**Approval outcomes:**

**✅ APPROVED:**
- Category added to master list
- Available to all businesses immediately
- You're notified via email

**✏️ MODIFIED:**
- Admin approves but changes the category name for clarity
- Example: Your suggestion "Computer Fixing" becomes "Computer Repair Services"
- You're notified with the final name

**❌ DENIED:**
- Category rejected with explanation
- Common reasons:
  - Too similar to existing category
  - Too narrow (only 1-2 businesses would use it)
  - Not a professional business category
  - Better fit as a secondary category under existing primary
- You can revise and re-submit

---

### Managing Multiple Categories

**Primary category selection:**
- Choose the ONE category that best describes your core business
- This determines your main position in search results
- Can be changed anytime (edit your listing)

**Secondary category limits:**
- Free Tier: 1 secondary category

**Adding secondary categories:**

- Think about how your customers look for services
- Example: HVAC contractor should add Electrical, Plumbing, not "Pet Grooming"

**What is a QR code?**
- A scannable barcode that links directly to your business listing
- Customers scan with their phone camera

**How to get your QR code:**

3. Click "Download QR Code"
4. Choose size:
   - Small (1 inch) - Business cards
   - Medium (3 inch) - Flyers
   - Large (6 inch) - Posters, window clings
5. Download PNG file
6. Print and use

**Where to use your QR code:**
- Business cards (back side)
- Flyers and brochures
- Window/door sticker
- Vehicle magnet or decal
- Email signature
- Social media graphics
- Yard signs
- Trade show booth

**QR code tips:**
- Print in high quality (300 DPI minimum)
- Don't shrink smaller than 1 inch (won't scan)
- Add text: "Scan to learn more" or "Scan for info"
- Test scan before mass printing

## Chapter 7: Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### Issue: My listing isn't showing up in search

**Possible causes:**
1. **Listing not approved yet**
   - Solution: Check email for approval notification, contact admin if it's been 3+ days

2. **Wrong category selected**
   - Solution: Edit listing, change to correct primary category

3. **Listing marked as inactive**
   - Solution: Dashboard → My Listing → Status → Change to "Active"

4. **Church not showing member businesses**
   - Solution: Verify church affiliation is saved, contact church admin

5. **Browser cache showing old results**
   - Solution: Clear browser cache or try incognito/private browsing

---

#### Issue: Payment failed during Pro upgrade

**Possible causes:**
1. **Card declined**
   - Check card expiration date
   - Verify billing ZIP matches card on file
   - Call your bank (may have blocked the charge as fraud protection)
   - Try a different card

2. **Insufficient funds**
   - Check account balance
   - Use different payment method

3. **Stripe system error**
   - Try again in 5 minutes
   - If persistent, contact support@kingdomconnects.com

4. **International card**
   - Currently only US cards accepted
   - Use US-issued Visa/MC/Amex/Discover

**What to do:**
1. Check email for failure notification (Stripe sends details)
2. Fix the issue (update card, add funds, etc.)
3. Try upgrade process again
4. If still failing, email support with error message screenshot

---

#### Issue: Can't upload photos/videos

**Possible causes:**
1. **Not on Pro tier**
   - Solution: Upgrade to Pro first (Chapter 4)

2. **File too large (over 10MB)**
   - Solution: Compress image/video before uploading
   - Use online tools: TinyPNG (images), Handbrake (videos)

3. **Wrong file format**
   - Images: Must be JPEG, PNG, GIF, or WebP
   - Videos: Must be MP4 or WebM
   - Solution: Convert file to supported format

4. **Browser issues**
   - Solution: Try different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
   - Update browser to latest version
   - Disable ad blockers

5. **Firebase Storage not enabled**
   - Contact admin if this is a new platform issue

**Upload tips:**
- Upload one file at a time for large videos
- Wait for each upload to fully complete before uploading next
- Don't close browser window during upload

---

#### Issue: Reviews not showing on my listing

**Possible causes:**
1. **Reviews pending admin approval**
   - Solution: Wait for admin to approve (usually 24-48 hours)

2. **Reviews were rejected**
   - Spam, profanity, or fake reviews get rejected
   - Legitimate reviews should be approved

3. **Allow Reviews setting turned off**
   - Solution: Dashboard → My Listing → Settings → "Allow Reviews" → Turn ON

4. **No reviews submitted yet**
   - Solution: Encourage customers to leave reviews
   - Add note on receipts: "Leave us a review on Kingdom Connects!"

---
