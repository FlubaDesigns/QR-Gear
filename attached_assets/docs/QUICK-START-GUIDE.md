**Version 1.0** | **November 2025** | **For All Users**

## 📖 Welcome to Kingdom Connects

### What is Kingdom Connects?

Kingdom Connects is a faith-based platform that unites churches with local Christian businesses. Our mission is to build **sustainable Christian commerce** by:

- Helping church members find and support local Christian-owned businesses
- Enabling businesses to connect with their faith community
- Returning 10% of platform revenue to churches and ministries
- Building economic strength within the Body of Christ

### How It Works

**For Businesses:**
1. Create a free listing in our business directory
2. Connect your business to your church
3. Upgrade to Pro ($9-12/month) for enhanced features
4. Get discovered by church members looking for Christian services

**For Churches:**
1. Create your church profile
2. Encourage members to list their businesses
3. Feature up to 5 member businesses on your church page
4. Receive 10% of revenue generated through your network

**For Regular Users:**
1. Browse businesses and churches in your area
2. Find Christian-owned services you can trust
3. Leave reviews and support the community
4. Help build the Kingdom economy

## 🔑 Getting Started: Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Sign Up

1. Go to **kingdomconnects.com/signup.html**
2. Fill in the required information:
   - **Full Name** - Your real name
   - **Email Address** - Used for login and notifications
   - **Password** - Must be at least 6 characters
   - **Phone Number** (optional) - For account recovery
3. Click **Create Account**
4. Check your email for a verification link
5. Click the verification link to activate your account

### Step 2: Verify Your Email

Within a few minutes, you'll receive an email from Kingdom Connects with a verification link. **You must click this link** before you can use your account.

**Didn't receive the email?**
- Check your spam folder
- Wait 5 minutes (sometimes emails are delayed)
- Click "Resend verification email" on the login page

### Step 3: First Login

1. Go to **kingdomconnects.com/login.html**
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **Sign In**
4. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

## 👤 Understanding User Roles

Kingdom Connects has **5 different roles**, each with specific permissions:

### 1. Regular User (Default)
**What you can do:**
- Browse businesses and churches
- View contact information and hours
- Read reviews from other users
- Create an account and manage your profile

**What you cannot do:**
- Submit your own reviews (coming soon)
- Create business or church listings
- Access dashboards

**This is the role you get when you first sign up.**

---

### 2. Business Owner (business-admin)
**What you can do:**
- Everything a Regular User can do
- Create and manage your business listing
- Upload photos and videos (Pro tier)
- Respond to customer reviews (Pro tier)
- View business analytics (Pro tier)
- Upgrade to Pro subscription
- Suggest new business categories

**How to get this role:**
Contact the Platform Admin to upgrade your account, then submit your business listing.

---

### 3. Church Admin (church-admin)
**What you can do:**
- Everything a Regular User can do
- Create and manage your church profile
- View all businesses affiliated with your church
- Feature up to 5 member businesses
- View church analytics
- Upload church photos and videos

**How to get this role:**
Contact the Platform Admin with proof you're authorized to represent the church (pastor, elder, admin staff).

---

### 4. Sales Agent (sales)
**What you can do:**
- Everything a Regular User can do
- Access sales dashboard
- Track referrals and conversions
- View commission earnings
- Monitor performance metrics
- Use unique referral codes

**How to get this role:**
Apply to become a sales agent. The Platform Admin will review your application and grant access if approved.

---

### 5. Platform Admin (admin)
**What you can do:**
- Everything all other roles can do
- Manage all users, businesses, and churches
- Approve/reject submissions
- View all platform analytics
- Manage payments and subscriptions
- Send email campaigns
- Handle category suggestions
- Access activity logs

**Who gets this role:**
Only Kingdom Connects staff and authorized team members.

## 🧭 Basic Navigation

### The Dashboard (After Login)

When you log in, you'll see your **role-specific dashboard**. Here's what each role sees:

**Regular User:**
- Currently redirected to homepage

**Business Owner:**

- Dashboard: Church profile overview, member business count

**Sales Agent:**

**Platform Admin:**
- Dashboard: Platform-wide metrics, pending approvals
- Pages: Users, Businesses, Churches, Reviews, Payments, Analytics, Email Campaigns, Activity Log, Settings

### Common Error Messages

**"Invalid email or password"**
- Double-check your email and password for typos
- Passwords are case-sensitive
- Use "Forgot Password" to reset if needed

**"Email not verified"**
- Check your email inbox (and spam folder)
- Click the verification link
- Wait a few minutes for email to arrive

**"Permission denied"**
- You're trying to access a page your role doesn't have access to
- Contact admin to upgrade your role if needed

**"Page not found (404)"**
- The page may have moved or been removed
- Use the navigation menu to find what you need
- Contact support if you believe this is an error

**Firebase error messages:**
- These are technical errors from our database
- Take a screenshot
- Email it to support@kingdomconnects.com
- Include what you were trying to do when the error appeared

---

### Password Reset

**If you forgot your password:**

1. Go to **kingdomconnects.com/forgot-password.html**
2. Enter your email address
3. Click **Send Reset Link**
4. Check your email (within 5 minutes)
5. Click the password reset link
6. Enter your new password (twice)
7. Click **Reset Password**
8. Return to login page and sign in with new password

**Didn't receive the reset email?**
- Check spam folder
- Wait 5 minutes
- Make sure you entered the correct email
- Try again or contact support
