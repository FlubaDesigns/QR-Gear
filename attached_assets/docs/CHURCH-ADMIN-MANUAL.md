## Chapter 1: Church Profile Setup

### Getting Church Admin Access

**Before you begin:**
- You must be authorized to represent the church
- Acceptable roles: Pastor, Elder, Church Administrator, Staff Member
- You'll need to provide proof of authorization

**Step-by-step process:**

1. **Contact Platform Admin**
   - Email: admin@kingdomconnects.com
   - Subject: "Church Admin Access Request - [Church Name]"
   - Include:
     - Church name
     - Your name and role at the church
     - Church phone number (for verification)
     - Your email address

2. **Verification**
   - Admin may call church office to verify
   - Or request documentation (staff badge, letter from pastor)
   - Verification typically takes 1-2 business days

3. **Account Upgrade**
   - Admin upgrades your account to "church-admin" role
   - You receive email notification
   - Log in to access church dashboard

---

### Creating/Editing Church Information

**To create church profile:**
1. Go to: kingdomconnects.com/submit-church.html
2. Or navigate to: Dashboard → Church Profile → Edit
3. Fill in all required fields (see below)
4. Click **Submit for Approval**
5. Wait for admin approval (1-3 business days)

**To edit existing profile:**
1. Dashboard → Church Profile
2. Click **Edit Profile**
3. Make your changes
4. Click **Save Changes**
5. Changes appear immediately (no re-approval needed)

---

### Required Church Information

#### ✅ REQUIRED FIELDS

**Basic Information:**
- **Church Name** - Full official name
- **City** - Church location city
- **State** - 2-letter code (e.g., FL, TX, CA)
- **ZIP Code** - 5-digit postal code
- **Denomination** - Your church's denomination/affiliation
  - Examples: Baptist, Methodist, Non-denominational, Presbyterian, etc.
  - Choose "Non-denominational" if you're not affiliated

**Contact Information:**
- **Contact Email** - Church office email (public)
- **Contact Phone** - Church office phone (public)

**Service Times:**
- **Sunday Service** - Time(s) for Sunday worship
  - Example: "9:00 AM and 11:00 AM"
- **Wednesday Service** - Mid-week service time (if applicable)
  - Example: "7:00 PM Bible Study"
- **Other Services** - Additional services, events, Bible studies
  - Example: "Youth Group Fridays 6PM, Men's Prayer Saturdays 8AM"

#### 📋 OPTIONAL FIELDS (Highly Recommended)

**Additional Information:**
- **Address Line 1** - Street address
- **Address Line 2** - Building number, suite
- **Website** - Church website URL
- **Description** - About your church (max 500 characters)
  - Tell visitors about your church's mission, vision, and community
- **Parishioner Count** - Estimated active members
  - Used for reach metrics and analytics

**Social Media:**
- Facebook page URL
- Instagram handle
- Twitter handle
- YouTube channel
- LinkedIn page

**Privacy Settings:**
- Show/hide social media links
- Allow reviews for church (coming soon)
- Allow social sharing

## Chapter 2: Managing Your Business Directory

### Viewing Member Businesses

**To see all affiliated businesses:**
1. Dashboard → Featured Businesses
2. Click **View All Member Businesses**
3. See complete list with:
   - Business name
   - Category
   - Owner name
   - Contact info
   - Pro status
   - Featured status

**Sorting and filtering:**
- Sort by: Name, Category, Date Added, Rating
- Filter by: Pro vs Free, Featured vs Not Featured
- Search by business name or category

---

### Featuring Member Businesses (Priority Listing)

**What is Featured Placement?**
- Highlight up to 5 member businesses
- Featured businesses appear at top of your church's business directory
- Gold border and "Featured" badge
- Increased visibility to church members and visitors

**Why feature businesses?**
- Reward active, engaged members
- Promote high-quality businesses
- Help members who support church financially/through service
- Build stronger church business network

**How to feature a business:**
1. Dashboard → Featured Businesses
2. Click **Manage Featured Listings**
3. See list of all member businesses
4. Click **Feature** next to business you want to highlight
5. Business immediately shows as featured
6. Repeat for up to 5 total businesses

**How to remove featured status:**
1. Dashboard → Featured Businesses
2. Find currently featured business
3. Click **Remove Featured**
4. Slot opens up for another business

---

### Featuring Guidelines

**Criteria for featuring:**
- Active church member in good standing
- Business provides excellent service
- Positive reviews and reputation
- Supports church mission and values
- Conducts business with Christian integrity

**What NOT to base featuring on:**
- Financial giving (don't "sell" featured spots)
- Personal friendships
- Pro vs Free tier status
- Length of church membership

**Fair rotation:**
- Consider rotating featured businesses quarterly
- Give opportunities to different members
- Don't feature same 5 businesses forever
- Balance categories (don't feature 5 plumbers)

**Handling requests:**
- Have a clear policy for featuring
- Communicate requirements to members
- Don't show favoritism
- If member asks to be featured, explain your process

---

### Encouraging Members to List Their Businesses

**Why it matters:**
- Builds church community
- Supports Kingdom economy
- Creates member-to-member business referrals
- Generates revenue sharing for church (10% of Pro subscriptions)

**How to promote:**

**1. Sunday Announcements**
"Attention business owners! Kingdom Connects is a Christian business directory where our members can support each other's businesses. If you own a business, create a free listing at kingdomconnects.com. See Pastor John for more info."

**2. Bulletin/Newsletter**
Include QR code linking to submit-business page  
Add short blurb about Kingdom Connects

**3. New Member Orientation**
Ask: "Do you own a business?"  
Provide Kingdom Connects brochure

**4. Business Owners Meeting**
Host quarterly gathering of church business owners  
Demo platform, answer questions  
Build community among Christian entrepreneurs

**5. Social Media**
Share your church's business directory link  
Highlight featured member businesses  
Tag businesses in posts

---

### Understanding the Church Affiliation System

**How affiliation works:**
1. Business owner selects your church from dropdown when creating listing
2. Business appears in your church's member directory
3. You can feature the business (up to 5 total)
4. Business owner gets benefit of church network

**Verifying member businesses:**
- Review list regularly
- Remove affiliations for non-members
- Contact platform admin to remove unauthorized affiliations
- Verify with business owner if unsure

**Multiple church affiliations:**
- Currently: Businesses can only affiliate with ONE church
- If member attends multiple campuses, choose primary

**Updating affiliations:**
- Member moves to new church: They update affiliation in their listing
- Business closes: Listing stays active but can be marked inactive
- Member leaves church: Contact admin to remove affiliation

## Chapter 3: Revenue Sharing

### Understanding the 10% Tithing Model

**Kingdom Connects' Mission:**
- Support Christian businesses financially
- Return 10% of gross revenue to churches and outreach programs
- Build sustainable Kingdom economy

**How it works:**
1. Business upgrades to Pro ($9-12/month)
2. Kingdom Connects receives payment
3. 10% of gross revenue allocated to:
   - Affiliated churches (5%)
   - Christian outreach programs (5%)
4. Monthly distribution to churches

**Example:**
- 100 businesses affiliated with your church
- 20 businesses upgrade to Pro at $12/month = $240/month revenue
- Your church receives 5% = $12/month
- All churches combined receive 5% = $12/month (distributed proportionally)
- Outreach programs receive 5% = $12/month

**At scale:**
- 1,000 businesses × $12/month = $12,000/month platform revenue
- Your church's share depends on:
  - Number of businesses affiliated with your church
  - Percentage of those businesses on Pro tier
  - Total platform Pro subscriptions

---

### How Revenue is Calculated

**Formula:**
```
Church Revenue Share = (Total Pro Revenue × 5%) × (Your Church's Pro Businesses ÷ All Pro Businesses)
```

**Example Calculation:**
- Total platform Pro revenue: $10,000/month
- Total allocation to churches: $500/month (5%)
- Your church has 10 Pro businesses
- All churches have 100 Pro businesses total
- Your church's share: $500 × (10 ÷ 100) = $50/month

**What counts as "Pro Revenue":**
- Monthly Pro subscriptions ($9-12/month)
- Annual Pro subscriptions (if offered)
- Does NOT include:
  - Ads revenue (future feature)
  - Premium church profiles (future feature)
  - Consulting/services fees

### Payment Schedule and Distribution

**When revenue sharing launches:**

**Monthly Distribution:**
- Calculation period: 1st - last day of month
- Processing: 1-5 business days after month end
- Payment method: Direct deposit or check

**Minimum payout:**
- $25 minimum per distribution
- If under $25, amount rolls over to next month
- No maximum limit

**Tax documentation:**
- Annual 1099 form (if over $600/year)
- Detailed transaction reports available
- Monthly statements for church accounting

**Setting up payment:**
1. Dashboard → Settings → Payment Information
2. Enter church bank account info for direct deposit
3. Or choose check mailed to church address
4. Verify information and save

## Chapter 4: Analytics

### Church Page Views

**What are page views?**
- Number of times your church profile page was viewed
- Includes both logged-in users and public visitors
- Tracked daily with historical trends

**Where to view:**
Dashboard → Analytics → Page Views

**Metrics shown:**
- **Total Views** (all time)
- **Last 30 days**
- **Last 7 days**
- **Yesterday**
- **Week-over-week change** (+/- percentage)

**What page views tell you:**
- How many people are discovering your church
- Impact of promotions (QR codes, social media shares)
- Seasonal trends (Christmas/Easter spikes)
- Whether your church profile is engaging

**Improving page views:**
- Complete all profile fields
- Add high-quality photos
- Share QR code in bulletin
- Post directory link on church website
- Feature member businesses (drives traffic)

## Chapter 5: Promoting Your Church

### QR Code for Bulletin/Website

**What is a QR code?**
- Scannable barcode linking to your church profile
- Members scan with phone camera
- Instantly opens church business directory
- Easy way to promote member businesses

**How to get your QR code:**
1. Dashboard → Church Profile
2. Scroll to "Marketing Tools"
3. Click **Download QR Code**
4. Choose size:
   - **Small (2 inch)** - Bulletin inserts
   - **Medium (4 inch)** - Posters
   - **Large (8 inch)** - Lobby displays
5. Download PNG file

**Where to use QR code:**

**1. Sunday Bulletin**
- Back page or inside flap
- Add text: "Support Our Member Businesses - Scan to Browse"
- Print in high quality

**2. Church Website**
- Homepage or "Ministries" page
- Embed image with link: yourchurch.kingdomconnects.com
- Add description: "Discover Christian businesses in our church family"

**3. Lobby/Welcome Area**
- Poster on welcome desk
- Table tent on info table
- Digital display (slide in rotation)

**4. Small Group Materials**
- Handouts for new members
- Small group leader packets
- Ministry leader resources

**5. Social Media**
- Facebook page post (pinned)
- Instagram bio link
- Twitter profile

**QR code best practices:**
- Print at least 2 inches square (smaller won't scan reliably)
- Test scan before mass printing
- Use high-quality printer (300 DPI minimum)
- Don't distort or stretch the code
- Include clear call-to-action text
