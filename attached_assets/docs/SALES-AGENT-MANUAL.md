## Chapter 1: Sales Agent Role Overview

### What is a Sales Agent?

**Kingdom Connects Sales Agent** is an independent contractor role focused on:
- Converting free business listings to Pro subscriptions ($9-12/month)
- Helping churches recruit member businesses to the platform
- Educating business owners about Kingdom Connects benefits
- Earning recurring commissions on Pro subscriptions

**This is NOT:**
- A full-time employment position
- A salaried role
- A franchise opportunity
- Multi-level marketing (MLM)

**This IS:**
- Commission-based independent contractor work
- Flexible schedule (work your own hours)
- Recurring passive income opportunity
- Faith-based business development
- Kingdom economy building

---

### Commission Structure

**Base Commission Rate:**
- **30% recurring commission** on every Pro subscription you sell
- Paid monthly as long as business stays Pro
- No caps or limits on earnings
- Commission continues even if you stop selling (passive income)

**Example Earnings:**

**Month 1:** Sell 10 Pro subscriptions at $12/month
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR): $120
- Your commission (30%): $36/month

**Month 2:** Sell 10 more (total: 20)
- MRR: $240
- Your commission: $72/month

**Month 12:** You've sold 100 total, 80 still active
- MRR: $960
- Your commission: $288/month

**Year 2:** No new sales, but 80 businesses stay Pro
- MRR: Still $960
- Your commission: Still $288/month (passive income)

---

### Payment Schedule

**Monthly Commission Payments:**
- **Calculation Period:** 1st to last day of month
- **Processing:** 1-5 business days after month end
- **Payment Method:** Direct deposit or PayPal
- **Minimum Payout:** $25 (rolls over to next month if under)
- **Maximum:** No limit

**Example Timeline:**
- January 1-31: You make 5 sales
- February 1-5: Kingdom Connects processes payments
- February 5-7: Your commission is calculated
- February 10: You receive direct deposit

---

### Terms and Conditions

**Independent Contractor Agreement:**
- You are NOT an employee of Kingdom Connects
- No benefits (health insurance, paid time off, etc.)
- You're responsible for your own taxes (1099 form provided)
- No exclusive territory rights
- Commission structure may change with 30-day notice
- Either party can terminate at any time

**Tax Implications:**
- You'll receive Form 1099-NEC if you earn $600+ per year
- You're responsible for self-employment taxes (~15.3%)
- Consider quarterly estimated tax payments
- Track business expenses (mileage, phone, marketing materials)
- Consult a tax professional

**Code of Conduct:**
- Represent Kingdom Connects honestly and ethically
- No false promises or misleading claims
- No spam or aggressive sales tactics
- Respect church leadership and relationships
- Conduct yourself as a Christian professional
- Violating code of conduct may result in termination and forfeiture of commissions

## Chapter 2: Finding Prospects

### Using the Platform to Identify Leads

**Dashboard → Referrals → Prospect Finder**

**Lead categories:**

**1. Free Tier Businesses (Hot Leads)**
- Already on platform but haven't upgraded
- They know about Kingdom Connects
- Lower friction to upgrade
- Filter by:
  - Church affiliation
  - Business category
  - Date joined (recent = hot)
  - Geographic location

**2. Churches Without Businesses**
- Churches on platform but low member participation
- Opportunity to educate pastor/admin
- Pitch church-wide campaign
- Filter by:
  - Parishioner count
  - Location
  - Denomination

**3. Categories with Low Representation**
- Healthcare providers
- Professional services (legal, accounting)
- High-value categories
- Less competition from other agents

---

### Church Directories

**Strategy: Work with Church Admins**

**Step 1: Identify Partner Churches**
- Dashboard → Churches → Browse All
- Look for churches with:
  - 100+ parishioners
  - Active church admin
  - Few or no member businesses listed
  - Your geographic area

**Step 2: Contact Church Admin**
- Introduce yourself and Kingdom Connects mission
- Offer to present at Sunday service or business owners meeting
- Provide free marketing materials (QR codes, flyers)
- Partner to recruit member businesses

**Step 3: Host Business Owner Meeting**
- Work with church to invite business owners
- 30-minute presentation:
  - What is Kingdom Connects?
  - Benefits of Pro tier
  - Live demo
  - Q&A
- Sign up businesses on the spot

**Step 4: Follow Up**
- Email attendees with your unique referral code
- Offer one-on-one setup assistance
- Check in weekly for first month

**Commission benefit:**
- One successful church campaign = 10-30 Pro sign-ups
- Example: 20 businesses × $12/month × 30% = $72/month recurring

- Track:
  - Prospect name
  - Business name
  - Phone/email
  - Contact date
  - Follow-up date
  - Status (cold, warm, hot, converted, lost)

## Chapter 3: Making Sales

### Free to Pro Conversion Process

**The Sales Funnel:**

**Stage 1: Awareness**
- Prospect learns about Kingdom Connects
- You explain benefits and mission
- Provide overview of Free vs Pro

**Stage 2: Interest**
- Prospect engages with questions
- You demo Pro features
- Show analytics, photos, examples
- Address objections

**Stage 3: Decision**
- Prospect weighs cost vs benefit
- You emphasize recurring value
- Offer limited-time incentive (if available)
- Ask for the sale

**Stage 4: Action**
- Prospect agrees to upgrade
- You provide unique referral code
- They complete checkout via Stripe
- Commission tracked automatically

**Stage 5: Retention**
- Check in after 30 days
- Ensure they're using Pro features
- Help with photos, analytics, categories
- Build long-term relationship

---

### Referral Tracking Codes

**Your Unique Referral Code:**

Every sales agent gets a unique code:
- **Format:** `SA-[FIRSTNAME]-[ID]`
- **Example:** `SA-JOHN-12345`

**How to use:**
1. Dashboard → Referrals → My Code
2. Copy your code
3. Provide to prospects during sales conversation
4. Prospect enters code during Pro upgrade checkout
5. Sale automatically attributed to you

**Where prospects enter code:**
- Pro upgrade page, before payment
- "Referral Code (Optional)" field
- If they forget, they can't add it later
- **Important:** Always remind them to enter your code!

**Tracking:**
- Dashboard shows all sales with your code
- See pending vs confirmed commissions
- Track conversion rate

**No code entered?**
- Sale is unattributed
- No commission earned
- **Always verify they entered your code before they checkout!**

---

### How Commissions are Calculated

**Base Formula:**
```
Monthly Commission = (Active Pro Subscriptions × Monthly Price) × 30%
```

**Example 1: Simple**
- You sell 10 Pro subscriptions at $12/month
- 10 × $12 = $120 MRR
- $120 × 30% = $36/month commission

**Example 2: Growth**
- Month 1: 10 sales, 10 active = $36 commission
- Month 2: 10 more sales, 20 active = $72 commission
- Month 3: 10 more sales, 28 active (2 cancelled) = $100.80 commission

**Example 3: Churn**
- You have 50 active Pro subscriptions
- 50 × $12 × 30% = $180/month
- 5 businesses cancel this month
- 45 × $12 × 30% = $162/month next month
- You lost $18/month recurring income

**Commission on Different Price Tiers:**
- $9/month Pro: $2.70/month commission
- $12/month Pro: $3.60/month commission
- Future enterprise plans: Higher commission rates

**Bonuses (Future Feature):**
- Reach 50 sales: +5% bonus on all commissions
- Reach 100 sales: +10% bonus on all commissions
- Top agent of the month: $500 bonus

```

MRR: $564/month

Lifetime Earnings: $1,521.60
```

---

### Pending vs Paid Commissions

**Pending Commissions:**
- Sales made but not yet paid out
- Waiting for monthly processing
- Shows estimated payout date
- Can change if business cancels before processing

**Paid Commissions:**
- Commissions already distributed to you
- Historical payment records
- Download monthly statements
- Tax documentation

**Dashboard → Commissions → Pending**

Example:
```
November 2025 Commission: $169.20 (Pending)
Estimated Payout: December 5, 2025

October 2025 Commission: $145.80 (Paid)
Payout Date: November 5, 2025
```

**Why pending changes:**
- Business cancels before month-end: Lost commission
- Business upgrades to higher tier: Increased commission
- Business downgrades: Decreased commission
- Refund issued: Commission deducted

---

### Monthly Statements

**Dashboard → Commissions → Statements**

**What's included:**
- All sales activity for the month
- New subscriptions (added)
- Cancelled subscriptions (lost)
- Net active subscriptions
- Commission calculation breakdown
- Payment method and date

**Sample Statement:**

```
Commission Rate: 30%
Gross Commission: $169.20
Adjustments: -$0.00
Net Commission: $169.20

Payment Date: December 5, 2025
Payment Method: Direct Deposit (Bank ****1234)
```

**Downloading:**
- PDF format for records
- CSV for import into accounting software
- Available for all past months

---

### Tax Documentation (1099 Info)

**Form 1099-NEC:**
- Issued annually if you earn $600+ per year
- Mailed by January 31
- Also available in Dashboard → Commissions → Tax Forms

**What's reported:**
- Total commission payments in calendar year
- Your name, address, SSN/EIN
- Kingdom Connects business information

**Important:**
- This income is taxable
- You're responsible for self-employment tax (~15.3%)
- Consider quarterly estimated payments to avoid penalties
- Track deductible expenses:
  - Mileage (IRS rate: 67¢/mile in 2024)
  - Phone/internet costs
  - Marketing materials
  - Home office (if applicable)
  - Training/education

**Tax Tips:**
- Use accounting software (QuickBooks Self-Employed, FreshBooks)
- Set aside 25-30% of commission income for taxes
- File Schedule C (business income) with Form 1040
- Consider S-Corp election if earning $50k+ per year
- Consult CPA or tax professional

## Chapter 6: Best Practices and Tips

### Effective Sales Techniques

**1. Lead with Mission, Not Money**

❌ Wrong Approach:
"Upgrade to Pro for just $12/month and get photos!"

✅ Right Approach:
"Kingdom Connects exists to support Christian businesses like yours. When you upgrade to Pro, you're investing in the Kingdom economy. Plus, you get features that help you attract more church-member customers—photos, videos, and analytics."

**Takeaway:** People buy into the mission first, features second.

---

**2. The Consultative Sell**

Don't pitch—ask questions first:
- "What's your biggest challenge in attracting Christian customers?"
- "How do you currently market your business?"
- "Do you track where your leads come from?"
- "Would it help to know your church members could easily find you?"

Then tailor your pitch to solve their specific problem:
- Challenge = visibility → Pro tier's priority placement and photos
- Challenge = reviews → Pro tier's review response feature
- Challenge = analytics → Pro tier's traffic tracking

---

**3. Social Proof**

Use testimonials and success stories:
- "ABC Plumbing upgraded 6 months ago and got 15 new customers through our platform."
- "Pastor John says Kingdom Connects has connected 30 member businesses at his church."
- "See this plumber's profile? He's getting 200 views a month. Yours could too."

**Create social proof:**
- Ask happy customers for testimonials
- Take screenshots of success metrics
- Build case studies of top businesses

---

**4. The Soft Close**

❌ Wrong:
"So, are you ready to upgrade now?"

✅ Right:
"Based on what you've told me, it sounds like Pro tier would help you reach more church members and track what's working. Does that sound like a good fit?"

**Then:**
- If YES: "Great! Let me walk you through the simple upgrade process."
- If HESITANT: "What concerns do you still have?" (Address objections)
- If NO: "No problem. Can I check back in 30 days?" (Stay in touch)

---

### Overcoming Objections

**Objection 1: "I can't afford $12/month"**

**Response:**
"I understand budget is important. Let me ask—if Pro tier brings you just ONE new customer per month, what's that worth to you? Most businesses say $100-$500. So $12 to potentially earn $100+ is a strong ROI. Plus, you can cancel anytime if it's not working. Want to try it for one month?"

**Alternative:**
"Think of it as $0.40 per day. Less than a cup of coffee to reach hundreds of potential Christian customers."

---

**Objection 2: "I already get enough business"**

**Response:**
"That's great that you're busy! Here's the thing—Kingdom Connects isn't just about getting more customers. It's about supporting the mission of a Christian business network. Your Pro subscription helps fund the platform and returns 10% to churches. Plus, when you're fully booked, you can recommend other Christian businesses in your network. It's about Kingdom building, not just your business."

**Alternative:**
"What happens in slower seasons? Having visibility here means you'll have a pipeline ready when things slow down."

---

**Objection 3: "I don't have time to manage another profile"**

**Response:**
"I totally understand. Good news—setup takes 10 minutes, and then it's zero maintenance. Your listing stays active 24/7, bringing you leads while you sleep. Pro tier adds photos once, and they work for you forever. Want me to help you set it up right now? I can walk you through it in one quick call."

---

**Objection 4: "I'll think about it"**

**Response:**
"Of course! What specific information would help you make a decision? Is it pricing, features, or something else?"

**Then address their concern directly.**

**Follow-up:**
"How about this—I'll email you a comparison chart of Free vs Pro. Can I check back with you in 3 days to answer any questions?"

**Set a specific follow-up date. Don't let "I'll think about it" become "never."**

---

**Objection 5: "I tried online directories before and they didn't work"**

**Response:**
"That's a common experience with big directories like Yelp or Angie's List. Kingdom Connects is different because it's faith-based and community-driven. Your customers here are church members actively looking for Christian businesses. It's a warm audience, not cold traffic. Plus, our churches promote this to their congregations. Have you seen how many members from [Their Church] are already here?"

### Building Long-Term Relationships

**1. The 30-Day Check-In**

After a business upgrades to Pro:
- Day 7: "How's it going? Need help uploading photos?"
- Day 14: "Have you checked your analytics yet? Let me show you."
- Day 30: "Let's review your first month. How many views? Any leads?"

**Goal:** Ensure they're using Pro features and seeing value. This reduces churn.

---

**2. Quarterly Business Reviews**

Every 3 months, reach out:
- "Let's look at your stats. You had 150 views last quarter—up 20%!"
- "I noticed you're not using all 5 category slots. Want to add more?"
- "How can I help you get more from Kingdom Connects?"

**Goal:** Show ongoing value, reduce cancellations, build loyalty.

---

**3. Referral Requests**

Happy Pro customers are your best source of new leads:
- "I'm so glad Kingdom Connects is working for you! Do you know any other Christian business owners who'd benefit?"
- "Would you be open to introducing me to your pastor? I'd love to present to your church."
- "Can I use your success story as a testimonial?"

**Incentive (Future Feature):**
- Referral bonuses: $20 credit for every referral who upgrades to Pro
- Your commission: Still earn on their referral sales

---

**4. Become a Trusted Advisor**

Go beyond selling:
- Share marketing tips
- Recommend tools (QuickBooks, Mailchimp, etc.)
- Connect them with other Christian business owners
- Pray for their business

**Result:** You're not just a salesperson. You're a partner in their success. They'll stay loyal and refer others.
