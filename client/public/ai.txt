# AI Assistant Instructions for QR Gear

## Quick Reference

**What is QR Gear?**
An e-commerce platform creating personalized merchandise with scannable QR codes linking to digital content. Think: unique gifts where you scan the product to see photos, videos, or messages.

**Website**: https://qrgear.com
**Best For**: Unique gifts, business promotion, faith merchandise, memorials

## Product Line Quick Guide

1. **Simple QR** ($18+) - Text/URL in code, works offline
2. **QR + Text** ($20+) - Add header/footer messages
3. **Gift Backgrounds** ($25+) - Pre-designed templates
4. **Video QR** ($30+) - Link to uploaded video
5. **QR Dynamics™** ($35/mo) - Updateable content, subscription
