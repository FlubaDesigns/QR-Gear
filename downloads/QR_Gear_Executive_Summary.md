# QR GEAR — Executive Summary

---

## What Is QR Gear?

Imagine you buy a t-shirt with a cool design on it. On that shirt is a small square code — a QR code. You pull out your phone, point your camera at it, and instantly a webpage pops up showing a video, a menu, a promotion, or whatever the shirt's owner wants you to see.

Now here's the twist: **the owner can change what that code shows — anytime, forever.** That shirt could show a summer sale today, a Christmas promotion in December, and a new product launch next spring. Same shirt. No reprinting. No new costs.

**That's QR Gear.** We make custom merchandise — shirts, mugs, hats, bags, and hundreds of other products — and every single one comes with a smart QR code that the owner controls.

We're not really a t-shirt company. **We're a marketing company that uses merchandise as the delivery vehicle.**

---

## How Does It Work? (The Simple Version)

Think of it in 4 steps:

**Step 1: Design It**
You pick a product (like a shirt), upload your logo or artwork, and our system places a QR code on it automatically.

**Step 2: We Print It & Ship It**
We don't keep any inventory. When someone orders, a printing partner prints it on demand and ships it directly to the buyer. We never touch the product.

**Step 3: The QR Code Goes Into the World**
Every time someone wears that shirt or drinks from that mug, the QR code is visible. Anyone with a smartphone can scan it.

**Step 4: The Owner Controls What Happens**
When someone scans the code, they see whatever the owner has set up — a website, a video, a photo gallery, a special offer. And the owner can change it whenever they want, as many times as they want, forever.

## How Does QR Gear Make Money?

We have **six ways** to earn revenue. Think of them like six different cash registers, all ringing at the same time.

---

### 1. Selling Products Directly

This is the simplest one. We sell QR-coded merchandise on our own website. We currently have **631 different products** — everything from $1.52 stickers to $162 premium jackets.

**How the money works:**
- A customer buys a shirt for $28
- It costs us $22 to have it printed and shipped
- We keep $6 profit

That's a 20% margin on every item. Not huge per sale, but it adds up — and every sale also activates a QR code (which feeds our other revenue streams).

---

### 2. QR Subscriptions (Monthly Recurring Income)

When someone buys a product, they get a basic QR code for free — it links to one thing and that's it.

But if they want the good stuff — rotating content, video, analytics showing how many people scanned their code, custom branded landing pages — they pay a monthly subscription.

**Pricing:**
- Basic: Free (gets people in the door)
- Plus: $2.99/month (rotating content, analytics)
- Canvas: $4.99/month (branded pages, galleries)
- Play: $7.99/month (video experiences)
- Pro: $14.99/month (the full suite)

**Why this is powerful:** Every product we ever sell creates a potential subscriber. A shirt sold in January could become a paying subscriber in October. And since the QR code is physically on a product someone owns, they don't cancel easily — it's not like canceling a streaming service you forgot about. It's on their favorite mug.

---

### 3. Creators Sell for Us (And We Split the Profit)

Anyone can sign up as a "creator" on QR Gear — an influencer, a small business owner, a church youth group leader, a school booster club parent.

They use our tools to design products, and we give them their own little storefront. When their products sell, we handle everything — printing, shipping, payments. They never touch a thing.

**The split:**
- We keep 75% of the profit
- They keep 25% — forever

So if a creator designs a shirt that sells for $30 and costs $24 to make, there's $6 profit. We keep $4.50, they get $1.50. Every single time that shirt sells. Forever.

**Why creators love this:** It costs them nothing to join. They carry no inventory. They just share their store link on social media and earn money whenever someone buys.

**Why we love this:** Every creator brings their own audience. We don't have to pay for advertising to reach those people — the creator does the marketing for us, and we only pay them when a sale actually happens.

---

### 4. Selling on Etsy, eBay, and Amazon

Millions of people shop on these marketplaces every day looking for custom and personalized products. We list our products there and let those platforms bring us customers.

We pay marketplace fees (6.5% on Etsy, up to 15% on Amazon), but we don't pay anything until a sale happens. It's free exposure with built-in buyer traffic.

**Bonus:** Every marketplace sale also creates a QR code instance — which can become a monthly subscription later (feeding revenue stream #2).

---

### 5. Businesses Use Our Platform (B2B)

This is the big-picture play. Instead of just selling shirts, we let other businesses use QR Gear as their own merchandise and marketing engine.

**Example:** A marketing agency wants to offer custom QR merchandise to their clients. Instead of building their own system, they plug into ours. Their brand, our technology. They pay us a monthly fee ($49–$199/month) plus a small cut of every sale.

**Other examples:**
- A church wants to sell branded merchandise with QR codes that link to sermons
- A real estate company wants "For Sale" signs with QR codes linking to virtual tours
- A restaurant wants branded merch with QR codes linking to their updated menu

Each of these businesses brings their own customers. We provide the infrastructure. They pay us every month.

---

### 6. The Referral System ("Share & Earn — Forever")

This is our secret weapon — and it replaces paid advertising entirely.

Here's how it works: If you refer someone to QR Gear and they buy something, **you earn 25% of the profit on that sale.** Not just once — **every time** that person buys, forever. And if that person refers someone else, you earn on those sales too.

**Why this matters:** Instead of spending money on Facebook ads or Google ads (where we'd pay upfront and hope for results), we pay commissions only when real sales happen. Our advertising cost is always exactly 25% of profit — never a penny more.

**The compounding effect:** Over time, referral chains grow. Person A refers Person B, who refers Person C. Person A earns a little bit every time any of them buy anything. This creates a growing network of people who are financially motivated to tell others about QR Gear.

## The Big Picture: Why This Works

1. **Every product is a permanent advertisement.** A shirt with a QR code doesn't stop marketing when the campaign ends. It keeps working as long as someone wears it.

2. **We only pay for results.** Referral commissions, marketplace fees, and printing costs all happen after a sale — never before. There's no gambling on ads.

3. **Revenue compounds.** Every product sold creates a QR code that can become a monthly subscription. Every creator brings new customers. Every customer can become a referrer. The system feeds itself.

4. **The market is massive.** We start with merchandise, but the QR technology works for restaurants, real estate, events, schools — anyone who wants to connect a physical thing to a digital experience.

5. **Six income streams, not one.** If any single channel slows down, five others keep going. That's built-in safety.
